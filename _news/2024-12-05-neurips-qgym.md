Excited to present our work **QGym: Scalable Simulation and Benchmarking of Queuing Network Controllers** at NeurIPS 2024! 

QGym provides a comprehensive benchmarking framework for queueing network control policies, addressing unique challenges in continuous-time systems with high stochasticity. Our framework includes diverse environments from parallel servers to realistic hospital queueing systems.

Thanks to all collaborators: Haozhe Chen, Ethan Che, Tianyi Peng, Jing Dong, and Hongseok Namkoong!
